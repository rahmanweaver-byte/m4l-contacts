Resources
Marine For Life Network (Official)
The national program page. Marines, veterans, and family members can request a referral to their regional representative — that's me for SoCal/Riverside — plus access to the M4L LinkedIn group and hiring events.
VA — Benefits, Healthcare & Claims
Start here for healthcare enrollment, disability claims, education benefits, and home loans. Set up a VA.gov account with Login.gov or ID.me and you can file claims, track status, and pull your own records in one place.
CalVet — California Veteran Benefits
California benefits stacked on top of federal ones: property tax exemptions for disabled veterans, college fee waivers for dependents, veterans homes, and CalVet home and farm loans. California residency required.
DoD SkillBridge — Transition Internships
Train with a civilian employer during your last 180 days on active duty while still drawing full pay, BAH, and TRICARE. Commander approval is required and every branch now caps the length by rank, so open the conversation 9 to 12 months out. Drilling reservists generally don't qualify unless on 180+ day Title 10 orders.
VR&E — Veteran Readiness & Employment
Chapter 31. Tuition, training, and job placement for veterans with a service-connected disability that gets in the way of working. Broader than the GI Bill — it can fund retraining, equipment, or self-employment — and it doesn't spend your GI Bill months.
Workshops for Warriors — Job Training
A nonprofit school at 2970 Main Street in San Diego running accelerated 16-week CNC machining and welding programs. Graduates earn credentials from the American Welding Society, NIMS, Mastercam, and SolidWorks. Open to veterans, transitioning service members, and wounded warriors; VA education benefits accepted. (619) 550-1620.
Unhoused Veterans
Homeless Veterans Call Line — 877-424-3838
Start here. Free, confidential, answered 24/7 by trained counselors who connect you to the nearest VA. Family and friends can call on a veteran's behalf. Tap to dial: 877-424-3838.
VA Homeless Programs — What's Available
The full picture in one place: outreach, emergency and transitional beds through Grant and Per Diem, and permanent housing through HUD-VASH and SSVF. Useful for figuring out which door you need before you call.
HUD-VASH — Housing Vouchers for Veterans
A HUD rental voucher paired with VA case management. Ask your local VA Medical Center's homeless coordinator to start a referral, or go through the call line above.
SSVF — Rent, Deposits & Rapid Re-Housing
Short-term help through community partners: back rent, security deposits, utilities, moving costs, and case management, either to prevent a loss of housing or to get you back into one fast. For rapid re-housing, VA states that employment, income, criminal history, and sobriety are not prerequisites.
U.S.VETS Inland Empire — Riverside
March Veterans Village at March Air Reserve Base, serving Riverside and San Bernardino counties since 2003. Emergency, transitional, and permanent housing plus mental health care and career services for more than 400 veterans and their families.
Veterans Village of San Diego
Veterans helping veterans since 1981. Emergency and transitional housing, treatment and recovery programs, and employment services out of their Pacific Highway campus. Serves veterans of all eras.
Father Joe's Villages — Housing & Services
San Diego's largest homeless services provider, based downtown. Shelter, interim and permanent housing, meals, medical and dental care, and job training. Their housing navigators can connect veterans into VA programs.
Veterans Justice Outreach
For veterans whose housing is blocked by court dates, an open case, or a record. VJO specialists link you to treatment and housing instead, and work with veterans treatment courts. Ask for the VJO specialist at your VA medical center.
Fun Benefits
Vet Tix — Free Concert & Game Tickets
Currently serving, Guard, Reserve, veterans of all eras, and families of the fallen. Create an account, get your service verified, then request tickets. You pay only a small delivery fee.
National Parks — Free Military Pass
Veterans and Gold Star Families get a free lifetime pass. Active duty, Guard, Reserve, and dependents get a free annual pass. Covers entrance at 2,000+ federal sites, including Cabrillo National Monument. Pick one up free at any park entrance with a CAC, VHIC, or DD-214, or order it from the USGS store.
SeaWorld San Diego — Waves of Honor
One free admission per year for active duty, drilling reservists, and Guard, plus up to three dependents. Veterans usually get their own free-ticket window around Military Appreciation Month in May — register early, it closes fast.
Blue Star Museums — Free Summer Admission
Armed Forces Day through Labor Day each year: active duty, Guard, and Reserve plus up to five family members get in free at 2,000+ museums, zoos, and gardens nationwide — most of Balboa Park included.
San Diego Zoo & Safari Park
Active duty enter free every day of the year with a valid ID — no ticket needed. Spouses and dependents get 10% off a 1-Day Pass. Retirees and veterans go through the MWR/ITT office on base instead.
San Diego Museums — Free & Reduced
USS Midway, the San Diego Air & Space Museum, and the Veterans Museum at Balboa Park are free for active duty and reservists year-round. Veterans get a reduced rate.
More San Diego Military Deals
Del Mar racing, LEGOLAND, Navy Getaways cottages, base golf, and the ITT ticket windows. Offers and dates change — confirm with the provider before you drive out.
